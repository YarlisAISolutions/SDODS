## The five-year horizon

SDODS climbs one ladder, and each year buys a level of confidence the year before could not.
2026 is delivered and verifiable today. 2027 is being built. The years after it are direction
rather than dated commitment, and feature requests move them.

| Year | Level              | What you get                                                      | Proof it landed                                             |
| ---- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2026 | 1 Evidence         | A release you can defend without being the person who remembers   | Fourteen phases delivered, published to npm                 |
| 2027 | 2 Shared truth     | One history the whole team reads, so no one relitigates a failure | A stable 1.0 with a compatibility promise                   |
| 2028 | 3 Self-maintaining | A suite that repairs itself and says what it changed              | Most locator drift merged without anyone writing a selector |
| 2029 | 4 Orchestrated     | Checks, approvals and the release in one place                    | The release gate becomes the record teams point at          |
| 2030 | 5 Governed         | An audit trail the people who sign off actually trust             | An auditor follows a requirement to a release unaided       |

Each year is written out with its customer value, the features that deliver it and its
milestone on the [roadmap page](https://sdods.com/roadmap/).
