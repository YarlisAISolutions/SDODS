## The road

Every stop on this road has a goal, the things that land there, and the one fact that tells you it
landed. Click a checkpoint to stand at it — Maxi will say why it is on the road at all. The stretch
behind the traveller is delivered, the twelve amber and indigo stops are the next year, and the
faint ones at the end are direction rather than dated commitment.

<Roadmap />

## Why the delivered stretch has no dates

The five chapters behind the traveller are real: the code is in the repository and the commands in
these pages run against it. They carry no month labels because this repository has no release
history to date them against — no tags, no published versions — and a date nobody can check is
worth less than an honest gap. When the first release lands (it is the November stop), the
chapters get the only date that means anything: the version they shipped in.

## Moving the road

The twelve months are shaped by what people ask for. The [feature request
link](mailto:admin@sdods.com?subject=%5BSDODS%20feature%5D%20) on any page moves an item up, and
the open gaps are listed on [Known limitations](/docs/reference/known-limitations). The phase-level
detail behind the delivered chapters, including the verification commands, lives in
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; the same five-year ladder is told for a non-technical reader on the
[roadmap page](https://sdods.com/roadmap/).
